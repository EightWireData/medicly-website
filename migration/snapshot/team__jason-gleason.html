Jason Gleason
CEO, Founder & Director

A business intelligence consultant with over 12 years of experience, Jason has always looked for better ways to deliver to his customers. His focus on continuous improvement for analytics delivery showed that a major issue was preventing complex customers from achieving their goals. The years of frustration took its toll so Jason founded Eightwire to build what his clients needed: a better way to share sensitive data across enterprise boundaries.

An all-round solution for sensitive health data transfer.

‍Medicly tackles the challenges that affect the time, energy, and cost of healthcare. It's easy to set up, operate, and maintain. It's secure and compliant, so you can focus on getting the valuable insights you need.
